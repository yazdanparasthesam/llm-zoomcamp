TripPilot audio briefings (multimodal extra)
============================================
Expected files in this directory (committed; regenerate with `make audio`):

  paris_vs_lyon_briefing.mp3        Paris vs Lyon food-focused city break
  barcelona_beach_city_break.mp3    Barcelona beach + city break
  eastern_europe_value_briefing.mp3 Prague / Budapest / Split value circuit

Scripts: data/transcripts.txt (source of truth, evidence-based)
Synthesis: Google TTS via `python3 data/generate_audio.py` (needs network).
The Streamlit app hides players if files are absent and still shows
transcripts — reviewers never hit a hard failure.
